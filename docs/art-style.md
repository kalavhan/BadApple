# Art style lock

Tone: dark and funny. Tim Burton crookedness, Don't Starve scribbly charm, a hint of Coraline button-eyed creepiness. Everything original.

## Shape rules
- Tall, thin, slightly crooked silhouettes; stick limbs; oversized heads and hands.
- Furniture and props lean, sag or tilt. Nothing perfectly straight or symmetrical.
- Faces are simple: big round eyes, small mouths, one funny detail per character.
- Monsters are funny at first glance and scarier on the second look.

## Pixel rules
- 16x16 tiles; characters about 2 tiles wide and 3 tall.
- 1 px ink outline, soft 3-tone shading, no anti-aliasing, no gradients; dithering only for textures.
- Warm candlelight in rooms, cold teal light in hallways.

## The Bad Apple
Glossy red apple, wide lopsided grin full of tiny teeth, half-lidded eyes with two highlights, one bent leaf curled like a devilish eyebrow.
